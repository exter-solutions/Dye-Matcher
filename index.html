Cold-Water Black Dye Tip-Loading Alert

In normal ambient water (25–30°C), high-molecular-weight Black dye strikes the outer suede nap tips and creates a cloudy charcoal frostiness. Keep Black below 30% of the mix, add Havana/Medium Brown to warm the core, or enable Neutral Grey above.
